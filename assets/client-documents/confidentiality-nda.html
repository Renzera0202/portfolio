CONFIDENTIALITY & NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

General template only; not legal advice. Have it reviewed for applicable privacy, employment, trade-secret, and cross-border laws before signing.

This Agreement is dated [Date] between [Disclosing party name] (“Disclosing Party”) and [Receiving party name] (“Receiving Party”). The parties are discussing or working together in relation to [Project / services].

1. Confidential Information

“Confidential Information” means non-public information disclosed in any form that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including business plans, customer and supplier details, pricing, credentials, processes, financial information, marketing plans, documents, and personal data.

2. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information the Receiving Party can demonstrate: (a) is public through no breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully known before disclosure; (c) was received lawfully from a third party without confidentiality restrictions; or (d) was independently developed without use of the Confidential Information.

3. Duties of Receiving Party

The Receiving Party will use Confidential Information only for [Permitted purpose], protect it using reasonable care, disclose it only to authorized people who need to know and are bound by confidentiality duties, and not copy or use it for another purpose. The Receiving Party will not sell, publish, or exploit the information.

4. Security and incident notice

The Receiving Party will use reasonable security measures, limit access, avoid sending passwords in plain text, and notify the Disclosing Party promptly after discovering suspected loss, misuse, or unauthorized access. The parties will cooperate reasonably to contain and address the incident.

5. Required disclosure

If disclosure is required by law or court order, the Receiving Party may disclose only what is legally required and, where legally permitted, will give advance notice so the Disclosing Party may seek protection.

6. Return or destruction

On written request or when the project ends, the Receiving Party will return or securely delete Confidential Information within [Number] days, except copies required by law or retained in routine secure backups, which remain protected and are not restored except as necessary.

7. Term

This Agreement starts on the date above. Confidentiality obligations continue for [Number of years] years after disclosure or termination. Trade secrets and personal data remain protected for as long as applicable law requires or they retain protected status.

8. No transfer of ownership or license

All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. No intellectual-property license or ownership transfer is granted except the limited right to use information for the permitted purpose.

9. Remedies and governing law

The parties may seek remedies available under applicable law. Governing law and dispute forum: [Jurisdiction / forum].

5. Client responsibilities

The Client will provide accurate instructions, timely feedback, necessary content and lawful access to systems. The Client remains responsible for approving business decisions, reviewing deliverables, maintaining backups, and ensuring that instructions and materials supplied to the VA may lawfully be used.

6. Confidentiality and data security

Each party must protect the other party’s non-public information and use it only to perform this Agreement. The VA will use reasonable safeguards, avoid sharing credentials, and promptly notify the Client of a suspected unauthorized disclosure. A separate NDA or data-processing agreement may add further requirements. The Client should provide access using role-based accounts rather than sharing personal passwords wherever possible.

8. Independent contractor relationship

The parties intend an independent service-provider relationship and not employment, partnership, or agency. The actual legal classification depends on applicable law and the real working arrangement, not this label alone. Neither party may bind the other without written authority.

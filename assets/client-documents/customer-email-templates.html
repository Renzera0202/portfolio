Customer Support & Sales Email Templates

Copy-ready starting points. Personalize every message and follow the client's tone and policies.

1. New inquiry acknowledgment

Subject: Thanks for reaching out Hi [Name], Thank you for contacting us. I've received your message about [topic] and will review the details. I'll get back to you by [time/date] with an update. If there's anything else you'd like us to know, feel free to reply to this email. Best, [Your Name]

2. Follow-up after no response

Subject: Following up on [topic] Hi [Name], I'm following up on my previous message about [topic]. When you have a moment, could you let me know whether you'd like to proceed or if you need any additional information? Thank you, [Your Name]

3. Appointment confirmation

Subject: Confirmation — [Appointment Date] Hi [Name], This confirms your appointment for [date] at [time and time zone]. The meeting will take place via [meeting link/location]. Please let me know if you need to reschedule. Best, [Your Name]

4. Complaint acknowledgment

Subject: We're reviewing your concern Hi [Name], I'm sorry to hear about the issue with [brief description]. I understand how important this is. I've documented your concern and will [next action] by [time/date]. Thank you for giving us the opportunity to look into this. Sincerely, [Your Name]

5. Sales outreach introduction

Subject: Support with [relevant business task] Hi [Name], I noticed [genuine, relevant observation]. I help with [specific service] so teams can spend more time on [business priority]. Would a brief conversation next week be useful? If not, no worries. Best, [Your Name]

6. End-of-day status update

Subject: Daily update — [Date] Hi [Name], Completed: • [Task] • [Task] In progress: • [Task and status] Waiting on: • [Information / approval] Next: • [Next action and expected timing] Best, [Your Name]

WELCOME — LET'S WORK BETTER TOGETHER

A quick guide to starting a smooth, organized working relationship with your Virtual Assistant.

1. Before we begin

We will confirm the services, priorities, deliverables, deadlines, working hours, timezone, rates, payment schedule, and communication channel in writing. A signed service agreement and any required confidentiality terms should be in place before work starts.

2. How to share tasks

Please provide a clear task description, desired outcome, deadline with timezone, priority, reference materials, and the person who will approve the work. Use one agreed task-management channel to avoid missed instructions.

3. Communication and availability

Primary channel: [Add channel]. Working schedule: [Add days / hours / timezone]. Expected response time: [Add expectation]. Urgent requests should be clearly marked and discussed in advance. Messages outside agreed hours will normally be handled during the next working period.

4. Tools and access

List approved tools and provide individual, role-based access wherever possible. Enable multi-factor authentication. Never send passwords or one-time security codes in an ordinary message. Remove access when it is no longer required.

5. Reviews and approvals

Deliverables will be shared through [platform / folder]. Please provide consolidated feedback within [number] business days. Approval should be explicit before content is published, messages are sent at scale, or irreversible changes are made.

6. Time tracking and invoices

Time / progress reporting method: [Tool / format]. Invoice frequency: [Frequency]. Payment terms: [Due date / number of days]. Any additional work or expenses must be approved in writing first.

7. Confidentiality and data handling

Client information will be used only for the agreed work and handled with reasonable safeguards. Please identify any special privacy, retention, or security requirements before sharing personal or regulated data. Do not send information that is unnecessary for the task.

8. Changes and support requests

If priorities, deadlines, or scope change, we will confirm the change and any impact on timing or fees in writing. Out-of-scope work will not begin until approved.

9. First-week checklist

  • Confirm signed agreement and scope of work
  • Confirm communication channel and working schedule
  • Share task priorities and reference materials
  • Set up secure access to required tools
  • Agree reporting and approval process
  • Confirm invoice and payment details privately
